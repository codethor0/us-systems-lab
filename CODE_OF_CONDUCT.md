# Code of Conduct

US Systems Lab is maintained by one person and welcomes issues, discussion, and
pull requests from anyone acting in good faith.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful and specific. Critique code, data, and methods, not people.
- Support claims about the model or its sources with evidence or citations.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what is merged, and why, in the open.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Repeated off-topic or bad-faith activity, including political campaigning in
  issues or pull requests. The model is illustrative; debates about policy
  belong elsewhere.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, and pull requests,
and may block accounts that violate this code. Report conduct concerns
privately to codethor@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports; follow SECURITY.md instead.
